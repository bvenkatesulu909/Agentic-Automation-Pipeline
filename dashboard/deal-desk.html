Two rules override the table. Annual contract value above ₹50,00,000 requires Finance at any discount, including 0%. And a discount that puts gross margin below 35% cannot be approved by anyone — the deal has to be restructured, not re-signed.

A discount that drops the net fee under the ₹75,000 monthly floor is a floor breach: allowed, but only with Finance on top of the band approver.

The agents behind this Nine agents. The dashboard reimplements their arithmetic; the agents add judgment, retrieval, and review.

These are per-vCPU-hour and per-GiB-hour rates, not machine prices. The catalog holds no per-machine-type SKU at all, which is why shared-core types like e2-medium — billed at a fraction of a vCPU — get overstated by anything that multiplies the core rate by the advertised vCPU count.

Four families have no committed-use SKU in this catalog. The agents report that as an answer rather than substituting a remembered CUD percentage, and so does this page.

Rates are a snapshot, not live. This page makes no network calls — the figures were read from the Cloud Billing catalog at the timestamp above and baked in. Re-run the extractor to refresh them.
The commercial rules are this repository's example policy. The 18% fee, ₹75,000 floor, 45% cost-to-serve, 35% margin floor and discount bands come from docs/pricing-and-discounts.md and the constants in adk_apps/sales_app/tools.py. Replace both with your own before quoting anything to a customer.
Free-tier allowances are deliberately never applied: Always Free is one allowance per billing account, and customer workloads run on ours.
